## Frequently asked questions
### What is competitive intelligence in B2B SaaS?
Competitive intelligence is the ongoing practice of gathering and analysing information about competitors, such as positioning, features, pricing signals, reviews and go-to-market moves, and turning it into decisions and sales tools like battle cards.

### What should a sales battle card include?
A good battle card includes when you win and lose against that competitor, their key claims and how to counter them, proof points, landmine questions to ask the buyer, and a one-sentence "why us" answer.

### How many competitors does the analysis cover?
Up to 5 in depth, and up to 15 in the landscape scan.

### Where does the data come from?
Public sources (websites, reviews, pricing pages, job posts, search data) combined with your win/loss notes.

### Can my team run it themselves later?
Yes. The AI research workflow can be handed over or hosted for you.
