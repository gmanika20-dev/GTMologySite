## You might be seeing
- People copying data between the CRM, spreadsheets and tools by hand
- Leads waiting hours or days to be routed or followed up
- Recurring reports and newsletters eating a day every week
- A tool stack that's grown, but nothing talks to anything else

## What's usually happening
Processes grew one task at a time. Nobody owns the "plumbing," so smart people end up doing robot work, and things fall through the cracks.

## How we do it
1. Map workflows: who does what, how often, and how long it takes
2. Score each by hours saved × impact × ease
3. Design the automations, with human approval steps where judgement matters
4. Build, test and document them (n8n, Make, Zapier, HubSpot, Clay, Copilot Studio)
5. Train your team and measure the hours saved

## What you get
Workflow & time audit · Automation opportunity map (ranked) · 3–5 built automations · CRM & lead-routing workflows · Enrichment & scoring setup · Documentation & runbooks · Hours-saved tracker

**Proof:** I automated recurring content audits, newsletters and reporting with n8n and Microsoft Copilot Studio, and connected campaign tracking to CRM and scheduling systems for automated reporting.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is GTM engineering?
GTM engineering is the practice of designing and building the technical systems behind go-to-market, such as data enrichment, lead routing, CRM workflows, reporting pipelines and AI automations. It lets marketing and sales teams scale without adding headcount.

### What marketing tasks can be automated?
Common candidates are lead routing and enrichment, follow-up sequences, recurring reports, content audits, newsletter assembly, CRM data hygiene and meeting preparation briefs.

### Is my data safe with GTM automation?
Yes. Automations run in your accounts, follow least-privilege access, and are fully documented.

### What happens if a tool in the automation changes?
Retainers include monitoring and fixes.

### Do we need technical staff to run the automations?
No. We hand over no-code-friendly workflows and runbooks.
