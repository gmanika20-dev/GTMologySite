## You might be seeing
- Every rep pitching the product differently

## What's usually happening
Sales materials are usually made one request at a time, without a shared view of the buyer, the competition or the sales process. Reps end up building their own versions, and what they learn in deals never makes its way back into marketing.

## How we do it
1. Review won and lost deals, call notes and current sales materials

3. Build sales plays for each priority segment and use case
4. Create battle cards and objection handling from competitive intelligence
5. Write the pitch narrative and update decks, one-pagers and email templates
6. Train the team and set up a simple way to keep content current

## Frequently asked questions
### What is sales enablement?
Sales enablement is the work of giving sales teams the knowledge, content, tools and training they need to sell effectively, such as sales plays, battle cards, pitch decks and objection handling.

### What is a sales play?
A sales play is a repeatable approach for a specific situation, such as a target segment, use case or competitor. It sets out who to target, what problem to lead with, the key messages and the next steps.

### What is a battle card?
A battle card is a short guide that helps reps compete against a specific competitor. It covers how they position themselves, where you win and lose, and how to respond to common claims.

### Do you train the sales team?
Yes. Every engagement includes a working session with the team, so the materials are understood and actually used.

### Can you use AI to keep enablement content current?
Yes, as an added capability. We can set up AI-assisted research and alerts that flag competitor changes, so battle cards stay up to date.
