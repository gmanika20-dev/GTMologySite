## Frequently asked questions
### What is conversion rate optimization (CRO)?
CRO is the practice of increasing the percentage of website visitors who take a desired action, such as booking a demo, by improving messaging, page structure, calls to action and user experience, and validating changes through testing.

### How do I get my website cited by ChatGPT or other AI assistants?
Publish clear, answer-first content on your core topics. Use structured data and consistent facts about your company. Build credible third-party mentions. Make sure AI crawlers are allowed to access your site.

### Which website platforms do you work with?
