# How do I launch a product that actually sells?
> **In short:** A product launch strategy, sometimes called a go-to-market launch plan, decides who a new product or feature is for, why they should care, how it is packaged, which channels will reach them and how sales will sell it. It turns a release date into a commercial plan with clear owners and success measures.

## You might be seeing
- Launches that ship on time but go quiet a week later
- Product, marketing and sales each with a different idea of who the launch is for
- Sales teams hearing about new features from customers first
- New products added to the website with no clear story of why they matter
- No agreed way to tell whether a launch worked

## What's usually happening
Launches are often treated as a release event instead of a go-to-market decision. The product is ready, but the target segment, the value message, the packaging and the sales enablement were never decided together, so every team improvises.

## How we do it
1. Define the launch goal, the target segment and the buyer problem the launch solves
2. Build the value proposition and positioning for the launch, tested against competitors
3. Decide the offer: which plan or package it belongs to and how it is introduced to existing customers
4. Plan channels and content: website, campaigns, email, partners and PR
5. Prepare sales: launch brief, pitch narrative, battle card and objection handling
6. Agree launch metrics, then review results and adjust after launch

## Frequently asked questions
### What is a product launch strategy?
A product launch strategy is the plan for bringing a new product, feature or plan to market. It covers the target segment, positioning and messaging, packaging, channels, sales enablement and how success will be measured.

### What is the difference between a launch plan and a go-to-market strategy?
A go-to-market strategy covers how the whole company reaches and wins customers. A launch plan applies that thinking to one specific product, feature or market entry, with a fixed timeline.

### Do you help with launches into new regions?
Yes. Entering a new market follows the same steps, with extra work on local buyers, competitors and channels.

### Can you help with smaller feature launches too?
Yes. We use a lighter version of the plan for features, so every release still has a clear audience and message.

### Do you create the launch assets as well?
Yes. We can write and produce the launch messaging, web copy, sales kit and campaign briefs, or guide your team and agencies to do it.
