# Who is my audience?
> **In short:** An ideal customer profile (ICP) defines the companies most likely to buy, succeed and stay with your product. Buyer personas describe the people on the buying committee. Together they decide who marketing targets, what sales prioritises and how you message.

## You might be seeing
- "Everyone could use our product," so marketing targets no one
- Leads that look right on paper but never close
- Long sales cycles caused by the wrong stakeholders
- High churn in some segments and great retention in others

## What's usually happening
ICPs are often written from assumptions rather than evidence. The best-fit customers are already hiding in your CRM, and in the patterns of who closes fastest, pays most and churns least.

## How we do it
1. Analyse closed-won, closed-lost and churned accounts
2. Interview 5–8 customers and 2–3 lost prospects
3. Score segments by fit, urgency, value and reachability
4. Map the buying committee, their triggers, objections and the questions they ask
5. Translate into targeting rules for ads, outbound and content

## Frequently asked questions
### What is an ideal customer profile?
An ideal customer profile (ICP) describes the type of company most likely to buy, succeed with and stay with your product, defined by firmographics, situation, triggers and buying behaviour. Personas then describe the individual people involved in the purchase.

### What is the difference between an ICP and a buyer persona?
An ICP describes the ideal company. A buyer persona describes an individual on that company's buying committee, such as the economic buyer, the champion or the technical evaluator, with their goals, concerns and questions.

### We're early and have few customers. Can we still do this?
Yes. With little data we lean more on market research, competitor customer bases and interviews.

### Do you run the customer interviews for the ICP?
Yes, and you get the recordings plus a written summary of what we learned.

### Can you build an ICP for multiple products?
We build one ICP per product line, or one per motion if the products share buyers.
