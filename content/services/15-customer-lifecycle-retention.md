# How do I keep and grow the customers I already have?
> **In short:** Customer lifecycle and retention marketing, also called customer base management, helps you keep customers longer and grow their accounts. It covers onboarding, product adoption, renewal and expansion through cross-sell and upsell, so more revenue comes from customers you have already won.

## You might be seeing
- Customers who sign up but never fully use the product
- Renewals that come as a surprise, in either direction
- Marketing focused almost entirely on new customers
- No structured way to introduce existing customers to other products or plans
- Customer emails that are generic, or that stop after onboarding

## What's usually happening
Most marketing budgets and attention go to acquisition, while the customer journey after the sale is left to chance. Without clear lifecycle stages, signals and plays, adoption stalls and expansion opportunities are missed.

## How we do it
1. Map the customer lifecycle from onboarding to adoption, renewal and expansion
2. Identify the signals of healthy, at-risk and ready-to-expand accounts
3. Design onboarding and adoption journeys across email, in-product messages and success touchpoints
4. Build cross-sell and upsell plays with sales and customer success
5. Set up automation in your CRM and marketing tools
6. Report on adoption, retention and expansion, and refine the journeys over time

## Frequently asked questions
### What is lifecycle marketing in B2B SaaS?
Lifecycle marketing is communication planned around each stage of the customer relationship, from onboarding and adoption to renewal and expansion, so customers get the right message at the right time.

### What is customer base management?
Customer base management is a structured approach to keeping, developing and growing existing customers. It includes segmentation of the customer base, retention programmes and cross-sell and upsell strategies.

### What is the difference between cross-selling and upselling?
Upselling moves a customer to a higher plan or more usage of the same product. Cross-selling introduces them to an additional product or module.

### Which tools do you work with?
We work with the CRM and marketing automation tools you already use, such as HubSpot, Salesforce and customer engagement platforms.

### Does this replace customer success?
No. It supports customer success with journeys, content and signals, so the team can focus their time on the accounts that need it most.
