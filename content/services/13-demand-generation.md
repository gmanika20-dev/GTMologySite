# How do I turn marketing spend into pipeline?
> **In short:** Demand generation is the work of creating and capturing interest from the right buyers and turning it into sales opportunities. For B2B SaaS it means running paid search, paid social, SEO, content and email as one integrated plan, with budget moved towards the channels that create qualified pipeline.

Channels are optimised for their own numbers, such as clicks, impressions and form fills, instead of for the shared goal of qualified pipeline. Without one plan built around the buyer, spend spreads thin and nobody can see what is really working.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is demand generation in B2B SaaS?
Demand generation is the set of marketing activities that create interest from the right buyers and turn it into sales pipeline. It includes paid media, SEO, content, email, events and the website, planned together around one target audience.

### What is the difference between demand generation and lead generation?
Lead generation focuses on collecting contact details. Demand generation focuses on creating real buying interest and qualified opportunities, which is what sales and revenue depend on.

### Which channels do you work with?
Mainly Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, SEO and AI search visibility, content and email, the channels that work best for B2B SaaS buying committees.

### Do you manage the campaigns or only plan them?
Both. We can run campaigns on a retainer, or plan them and guide your team or agency.

### How do you decide where the budget goes?
We set performance benchmarks for each campaign and move spend towards what creates qualified pipeline, reviewing it on a regular cycle.
