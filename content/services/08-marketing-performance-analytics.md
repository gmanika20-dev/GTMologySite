## Frequently asked questions
### What is marketing funnel analysis?
Marketing funnel analysis measures how many prospects move from each stage to the next (visit, lead, MQL, opportunity, pipeline, revenue) to find where the biggest drop-offs occur and why.

### What should a B2B marketing dashboard show?
It should show pipeline created and influenced, conversion rates between funnel stages, cost per opportunity by channel, velocity, and trends over time, with a short commentary on what changed and what to do next.

### Which analytics tools do you work with?
GA4, GTM, HubSpot, Salesforce, Power BI, Looker Studio, Excel, and whatever you already have.

### Do we need clean data before starting marketing analytics?
No. We start with what exists and fix as we go.
