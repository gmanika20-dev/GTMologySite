# How should my brand sound?
> **In short:** A brand voice is the consistent personality in how a company writes and speaks, across the website, sales emails, LinkedIn, product UI and AI-generated content. Good voice guidelines make every writer, and every AI tool, sound like the same trusted expert.

## You might be seeing
- Content that sounds generic, or like it was written by five different people
- AI-generated drafts that need heavy rewriting
- A founder voice that doesn't scale beyond the founder
- Sales emails, website and social that feel like different companies

## What's usually happening
Voice lives in a few people's instincts and never gets written down in a usable way. AI tools then default to bland "marketing speak," because nobody has taught them who you are.

## How we do it
1. Audit your existing content, plus the voices of competitors and customers
2. Define voice attributes as "this, not that" spectrums
3. Set tone by channel and context (website, sales, social, support, crisis)
4. Build a vocabulary of words you use, words you avoid, and how you name product terms
5. Package it as an **AI-ready voice prompt** for Claude, ChatGPT and Copilot

## Frequently asked questions
### What is a brand voice?
A brand voice is the distinct and consistent personality a company uses in all written and spoken communication. It is defined by voice attributes, tone that adapts to context, and vocabulary rules.

### How do you make AI write in your brand voice?
Codify the voice into an AI system prompt with attributes, examples of on-brand and off-brand writing, vocabulary rules and context about your positioning. Then load it into the AI tools your team uses as a project or custom assistant.

### Is this only for marketing?
No. Sales, customer success and product UI copy benefit most from consistency.

### Do you set up our AI tools?
Yes. The prompt pack and setup are included.

### Can we keep the founder's voice?
Yes. We codify it so others can write in it.
